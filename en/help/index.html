Help

Didn’t find an answer? Write to support@autario.app. We usually reply within 1–2 working days, in English or Polish.

The registration document scan doesn’t read my document

  • Photograph the page with the vehicle details flat, in good light, filling most of the frame.
  • Avoid glare on plastic or laminated documents: tilt the document slightly.
  • Always check the fields before using them; you can also enter the details manually.
  • In Poland the app reads the Aztec code on the registration certificate instead: hold the phone 15–20 cm away with the code in the frame.

The deadlines don’t match my country

Set your country in Settings → Country. Autario then offers that country’s deadlines (for example MOT and VED in the UK, registration renewal in the US, HU (TÜV) in Germany, ITV in Spain) and uses its currency and units for new cars.

I don’t get reminders

  • Check that notifications are on: Settings in the app → Notifications → Notification settings.
  • Reminders arrive at 9:00 on the days set for the deadline (by default 30, 14 and 1 day before). You can also add a deadline to your calendar from its form.

New phone: how do I move my data?

On the old phone: Settings → Backup → Export data and save the file (for example in iCloud Drive). On the new one: Settings → Backup → Import data. Cars in a shared garage appear by themselves after you sign in.

I bought Pro but don’t see it

Settings → Autario Pro → Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple ID used for the purchase.

How do I cancel Pro or Family?

On your iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Autario. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period.

Shared garage

  • The owner invites people with a QR code or a link (valid for 7 days, works once).
  • Roles: an editor can add and change data, a viewer can only look.
  • Joining someone else’s garage from an invitation is free.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Account → Delete account. Data saved on your phone stays; we delete the account and the shared garages you own (or you hand them over). More in the privacy policy.
