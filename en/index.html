FAQ

Questions

Is Autario free?

Yes. For free you keep one car with deadlines, reminders, a service log and expenses. Pro (14 days free) unlocks more cars, scans, charts and the PDF report, and the Family plan adds a shared garage. Prefer to pay once? There is also Pro Lifetime.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without an account and offline. You only sign in (with Apple or Google) to share a garage with your family.

Where is my data stored?

On your phone. Only cars you share in a family garage are synced to a server in the European Union. Details in the privacy policy.

How does the registration scan work?

Take a photo of your registration document. Autario reads the printed fields on your iPhone and fills in the make, model, VIN, year and plate for you to check. The photo is not stored or uploaded, and we do not read the owner’s details.

Which deadlines does it know?

It adapts to your country: insurance, registration renewal and inspection in the US and Canada; MOT, VED and insurance in the UK; NCT and road tax in Ireland; plus any custom deadline by date or mileage.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

In Settings, export a backup (one file with your cars, entries and photos) and import it on the new phone. In a family garage your data syncs by itself after you sign in.
