Privacy policy

Effective 13 November 2026, updated 18 November 2026. This is the English version of the Polish policy; it applies to users in every country.

Autario is an app for looking after your cars: insurance, registration and inspection deadlines, a service log and expenses. The rule is simple: your data stays on your phone. Only what we describe below leaves it, and only for the purpose described.

1. Controller

The controller is Jakub Cienciała, ul. Adama Dzióbka 21d, 41-409 Mysłowice, Poland.

Contact about personal data: support@autario.app.

2. Data in the app (cars, deadlines, service, expenses, photos)

Everything you enter (cars, registration plates, VINs, mileage, deadlines, service entries, expenses, notes, photos of invoices and documents) is stored only on your phone. We have no access to it.

  • Scanning registration documents and insurance policies happens on the phone. The photo is not stored or sent anywhere. We read only the vehicle details; we do not read or store the owner’s details. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, the text of a scanned policy may be processed by Apple’s on-device language model; it does not leave the phone.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on the phone; they do not pass through our servers. Deadlines you add to your calendar are saved by you in the iOS calendar.
  • Backups are files you export yourself and keep wherever you choose.

3. Shared garage (Family plan)

If you sign in (with Apple or Google) to use a shared garage, we process:

  • account data: the identifier from Apple or Google, your email address (with Apple this may be a hidden address), your name if you provide it;
  • garage data: its name, members and their roles, invitations;
  • data of the cars you choose to share in the garage (including plates, VINs, deadlines, service entries, expenses, notes and photos), visible only to the members of that garage.

Cars you do not share never leave your phone. We store the data on Supabase servers in the European Union (Frankfurt). Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

4. Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple (App Store). To check whether you have Pro or the Family plan we use RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app identifier and the purchase confirmation from Apple. We never receive your card details. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

5. Crash reports

When the app crashes or an error occurs, we send a technical report to Sentry (servers in the EU): the error, device model, system and app version, screen names. The report contains none of your app data, plates, VINs, IP address or identity. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the app stable (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

6. Anonymous statistics (only with consent)

If you agree (we ask on first launch; change it in Settings → Privacy), we send PostHog (servers in the EU) anonymous usage events such as “car added” or “purchase screen opened”, with a random identifier, the app and system version and the device language. We do not send personal data, plates, VINs, amounts or text you typed, and we do not determine your location. Without consent nothing is sent. Legal basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time.

8. How long we keep data

  • Data on your phone: until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Account and shared garage: until you delete your account. Deleted entries may remain in technical backups for up to 90 days.
  • Crash reports (Sentry): up to 30 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (PostHog, only with your consent): up to 1 year, then deleted automatically.

9. Deleting your account

Delete your account in the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. We delete the account, your memberships and the garages you own (or you hand them over to another member). Data on your phone stays. Cancel any subscription separately in your Apple account settings.

10. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict processing, to data portability, to object to processing based on legitimate interest and to withdraw consent at any time. Write to support@autario.app. You may also complain to a data protection authority, in particular the one in your country of residence (in Poland: the President of the Personal Data Protection Office, uodo.gov.pl).

11. Children

The app is not directed at people under 16.

12. Changes

We will announce material changes to this policy in the app or on this page, with a new effective date.
